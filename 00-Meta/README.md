## What is FDSE?

A Forward Deployed Software Engineer is embedded directly with clients to understand their problems and ship solutions on-site — fast. It's part engineer, part consultant, part problem solver.

Think: **Palantir-style engineering**. You don't wait for a PM to write requirements. You go find them yourself.
